# 📊 Indian Mutual Funds Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing **2,098 active Indian mutual fund schemes** across **50 Asset Management Companies (AMCs)**. This project tracks Assets Under Management (AUM), expense ratios, scheme categories, and investment plan preferences (**GROWTH vs. IDCW**, **Direct vs. Regular**, and **ETFs**).

## 🚀 Key Highlights & Insights (Day 1 Progress)
- **KPI Metrics:** 2,098 Active Schemes | 50 Fund Houses | 0.33% Avg Expense Ratio | ₹242.16 Avg Latest NAV.
- **Plan Preference:** **GROWTH** plans dominate overall AUM compared to IDCW plans due to long-term compounding benefits.
- **Top AMCs by AUM:** **HDFC Mutual Fund (₹15.5K Cr)** and **ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund (₹14.5K Cr)** lead in the GROWTH segment, while **Edelweiss Mutual Fund (₹9.4K Cr)** leads in IDCW.
- **Top Categories:** **Index Funds** and **Other ETFs** hold the highest number of schemes, highlighting strong passive investing adoption.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filtering for **ETFs** and **Direct Plans**.
